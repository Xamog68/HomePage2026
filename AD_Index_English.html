This section collects course editions and short courses taught entirely in English.

Available courses

Calculus of Variations — 2016/17

English edition of the introductory course in the calculus of variations. The course develops the indirect and direct methods, one-dimensional Sobolev spaces, relaxation, second variation, calibrations and first examples of Gamma-convergence.

Principles of Mathematical Analysis — 2021/22

English edition of the fourth-year course on variational methods, Sobolev spaces in arbitrary dimension and classical functional analysis.

Freiberg 2017

Short PhD course on linear hyperbolic equations with time-dependent propagation speed and strong damping.
